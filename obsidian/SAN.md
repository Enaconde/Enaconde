# SAN

## Idea
A friend group creates content under one shared brand, **SAN**. Each person runs a vertical: SAN Information, SAN Gaming, SAN [something]. Goal: build influence with the younger generation.

## Mission (from the founder)
- Young Mongolians (18-25) tend toward "idk, we don't care". Many know more about America than about what is happening at home.
- Belief: this generation will live in the future and can fix Mongolia's problems, but first has to know what is going on.
- Plan: gain influence first, then "maybe..." (thought unfinished, to be completed).

### What this means for the plan
- Apathetic audience will not watch a lecture. **Entertainment first, information hidden inside it.**
- "SAN Information" is probably the core vertical; gaming/lifestyle verticals can bring in the audience that info alone won't reach.
- Credibility is the asset: cite sources, separate fact from opinion, be careful about defamation. Not tied to a party.
- Make it personal and local: "what does this mean for your salary, rent, air pollution, tuition" beats abstract politics.
- Short formats: "Mongolia in 60 seconds", explainers, street interviews, comparing US news vs home news.
- Measure success by shares and comments, not only views.

## Honest odds
- Most creator channels never reach a meaningful audience. Odds of "big" are low for any single channel.
- Odds of **some** audience (thousands of followers) are decent if you ship consistently for 6-12 months. Most people quit before then.
- Biggest risks: friends losing interest, inconsistent quality, spreading thin across too many verticals.
- Biggest edges: several people means more output, cross-promotion, and a shared brand that compounds. Mongolian-language niches are less crowded than English ones.

## Plan
### Phase 0: Decide (week 1-2)
- [ ] Who is actually committed, and how many hours a week each?
- [ ] Pick **one** launch vertical, not all. Start with the one you can make 2x a week.
- [ ] Target audience: age, platform (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels / Facebook), language.
- [ ] One-sentence promise of each vertical, and what makes SAN different from existing channels.
- [ ] Agree on ownership: who owns the brand, accounts and money. Write it down now while everyone is friendly.

### Phase 1: Launch one vertical (month 1-3)
- Short-form first (Shorts/Reels/TikTok): fastest feedback, no big setup.
- Post on a fixed schedule. Target 30-50 videos before judging anything.
- Same brand name, handle, logo and intro across all accounts.
- Track per video: views, watch time, shares, follows. Keep what works.

### Phase 2: Prove it (month 3-6)
- Double down on the best-performing format.
- Friends cross-appear in each other's videos.
- Add a second vertical only once the first has a steady routine.

### Phase 3: Expand and earn (month 6+)
- More verticals, longer videos, community (Discord / Telegram).
- Monetise: platform payouts, sponsors, merch, local brands that want young audiences.

## Rules of thumb
- Consistency beats quality early. Quality comes from volume.
- Don't judge before ~30 posts.
- Brand over individual: audience follows SAN, not one person.
- Avoid clickbait that hurts trust; trust is the long-term asset.
